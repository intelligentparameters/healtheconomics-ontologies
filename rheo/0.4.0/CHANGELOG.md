## Why

The ontology pages on the site moved to https://healtheconomics.wiki/ontologies/<name>. The identifiers now match the pages. The old /ontology/ addresses are retired and are not redirected. The prior version identifier, https://healtheconomics.wiki/ontology/rheo/0.3.0/rheo.owl, is kept as published.

## Checks

Every file was compared with the previous release triple by triple after mapping the old addresses and versions to the new ones. Only the ontology header differs. The merged file was checked for consistency with the ELK reasoner.

## 0.2.1 (draft, 8 October 2026)

Fixes from the independent re-check of 0.2.0.
- **Ontology:** "merges states", so a later classification can merge earlier states; "made under agreement", linking a payment to the price agreement it was made under; "has flow reason", for flows to or from outside the system recorded. A flow measurement may now have an origin, a destination or both.
- **Stock-flow balance:** rewritten. Flows anywhere inside the period between two counts are counted. A flow that straddles a count date is flagged as a warning instead of switching the check off. A model's projected counts are balanced within the run that produced them. Projected counts now have the same date rules as observed ones.
- **RNI:** a later state is related back over several classification versions and through merged states. States of two versions of one classification recorded for the same entity on the same date must correspond.
- **Competency questions:** 13, 19, 25, 27, 28, 29, 33 and 37 aggregate each side on its own, so further valid records do not double count. 30 uses the payment's link to the agreement. 36 uses the latest recommendation by the date. 38 judges each option by its quadrant and net monetary benefit, not by the ICER alone. `run_demo.py` reruns eleven questions with further valid records added (`examples/cq-robustness-additions.ttl`).
- **Scenarios:**
  - S12 costs the programme as the ministry's spending, converted at the market exchange rate, and states that the comparison districts are used without adjustment.
  - S13 counts nurses as a headcount with full-time equivalents beside it, and uses plausible staff time.
  - S14 links the outcomes-based payments to the agreement.
  - S16 keeps the household's self-funded weeks out of the NHS and personal social services costing.
  - S18 records doses administered and wasted as outflows, and the people vaccinated as people.
  - The method text of C302 now matches its value.
- **Second re-check:** sibling states of two versions on the same date are now rejected; a move from a finer state to the coarser state it refines is replacement; CQ38 reports an ICER it cannot judge instead of dropping it, and matches price year; the extension directive classes are disjoint from the core ones; S14 payments use payment by outcome; the S17 encounter names its patient and provider.

## 0.2.0 (draft, 8 October 2026)

RHEO becomes a realist ontology of health economic reality, not only of the evidence chain.

- **Real-world module** (`src/reality_module.py`):
  - persons, households, organizations, roles (patient, provider, payer, informal carer, resource), care settings, geographic areas, resource stocks, workforces and member groups;
  - health care processes (reusing OGMS) and their kinds;
  - social and informal care, paid work, resource use, patient movements and payments;
  - behaviours, infection transmission, HTA and regulatory decisions, and death.
- **Records of the real world:**
  - state observations under versioned classification schemes (with lineage, refinements and absorbing states);
  - transition records classified by RNI (replacement, novation, innovation);
  - resource use and work absence records;
  - perspectives, costing and cost records;
  - case-mix grouping, tariffs, payment mechanisms and payment records, price agreements and outcomes-based agreements;
  - stock and flow measurements and projected counts;
  - household economic records and currency conversion;
  - test accuracy, recommendations, marketing authorisations, funding rules and decision thresholds.
- **Twelve extension areas** (`src/extensions_module.py`):
  - QALYs from records with discounting;
  - DALYs and cost per DALY averted;
  - workforce;
  - outcomes-based agreements;
  - trials with missing data;
  - social care;
  - insurance coverage;
  - vaccine supply and wastage;
  - carbon footprint (emissions records);
  - technology diffusion;
  - donor funding flows;
  - the institutional layer.
- **Twenty-one fictional scenarios**, 38 competency questions, and record rules with violations and warnings (warnings in their own shapes). Broken and legitimate edge-case records prove each rule.
- **No duplicate concepts:** RHEO_0000001 (health care process) is obsolete in favour of OGMS_0000096, and RHEO_0000045 (medical device) in favour of COB_0001300. Both are obsoleted to OBO conventions and listed in the mapping file.
- **Independent review, round 1:** the draft went through an independent review before the creators saw it. Everything the review required was addressed:
  - the domain leak that typed household records as model results;
  - an RNI rule that counted any scheme change as innovation;
  - stocks that counted descriptions instead of real groups;
  - projections typed as measurements;
  - a stock-flow balance that could pass by accident;
  - double counting and cross-joins in several competency questions;
  - several category choices.
  The changes are listed in the README and in `src/patch_0_2_review.py` and `src/patch_0_2_scenarios.py`.
- **Independent re-check:** stale definitions corrected, and the extension record classes made disjoint from each other, from the core records and from model results (`src/patch_0_2_recheck.py`). The remaining findings, in the example queries, record rules and scenarios, are listed under known limits in the README for 0.2.1.
