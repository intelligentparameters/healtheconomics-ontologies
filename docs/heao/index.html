Health Economics Ontologies

Health Economics Axiology Ontology (HEAO)

First release (status alpha) for review by its creators and three independent reviewers. HEAO imports OHEO 0.3.0 and RHEO 0.4.0 with its uncertainty module, which imports BFO 2020 (ISO/IEC 21838-2). The BFO, IAO, COB, OBI and PROV terms RHEO declares are reused and not declared again, apart from PROV-O was attributed to, which RHEO does not declare. Load heao-bridge.owl to see the OHEO and RHEO terms classified under HEAO. Term IRIs follow the family pattern https://healtheconomics.wiki/ontologies/heao/HEAO_ and seven digits, as RHEO, OHEO and HETO do, a choice made in the fix pass of 18 October 2026 for Darrin Baines to confirm.
