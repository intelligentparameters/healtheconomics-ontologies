Health Economics Ontologies

Health Economics Institutions and Rules Ontology (HEIRO)

First release, status alpha, revised in place after three independent reviews on 19 October 2026. HEIRO imports RHEO 0.4.0 (which imports BFO 2020), HEAO 0.2.0 as published (which imports OHEO 0.3.0 and the RHEO uncertainty module, and holds the five linking kinds), and an extract of IAO for document part. Load heiro-register.owl for the bodies, guidance documents, versions and rules, and heiro-wiki.owl for the records generated from the healtheconomics.wiki. Term IRIs are https://healtheconomics.wiki/ontologies/heiro/HEIRO_ and seven digits.

institutional role

https://healtheconomics.wiki/ontologies/heiro/HEIRO_0000001
Curation status pending final vettingexternal

A role borne by an organization that is realised in issuing, adopting or applying rules that set or limit the options other actors in a health system have, such as which technologies are funded, at what price, or by which methods they are evaluated.

Subclass of roleexternal
Subclasses health technology assessment role, methods guidance role
contributor Michelle Ashleyexternal
definition source Baines D. Institutional layer of the healtheconomics.wiki, unpublished notes of 3 and 4 October 2026 (institutions shape the options actors have)
North 1990
RHEO 0.4.0 (Baines and Ashley 2026)
editor note Institutions in North's sense are the rules, and organizations are the players. HEIRO keeps the two apart. A body is an organization that bears an institutional role, and the rules it issues are directive information entities. It is a role and not a kind of organization, so that one organization can hold several roles, as with RHEO's payer role. HEIRO holds formal written rules only. Norms and conventions, which are also institutions in North's sense, are out of its scope.
isDefinedBy heiroexternal
term editor Michelle Ashley (drafted with Claude)
