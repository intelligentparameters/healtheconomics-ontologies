Health Economics Ontologies

Health Economics Ontology (HEO)

A vocabulary of health economics concepts, with definitions and typed links between them, taken from HealthEconomics.wiki. Each concept is identified by a stable URI that serves as its permanent identifier. Concepts are SKOS concepts arranged by skos:broader under one top concept, Health Economics, and the wiki's subdomains; a concept with no is-type-of parent is placed in that subject structure with heo:classifiedUnder. Six relationships (part of, measures, used to calculate, treats, risk factor for, precedes) are OWL object properties with named inverses, and the others are descriptive annotation properties. HEO identifies concepts; its companion RHEO defines types classified within the Basic Formal Ontology. A concept and a type never share an identifier.

classified under

https://healtheconomics.wiki/ontologies/heo#classifiedUnder

Places a concept in the subject structure of HealthEconomics.wiki (Health Economics, its subdomains and the concepts under them) when the concept has no is-type-of parent. It is a placement for finding and browsing, not a claim that the concept is a type of the concept it sits under.

Subproperty of broaderexternal
application use navigation
example Literature Search classified under Evidence Synthesis
scopeNote Placement comes from the wiki's live topic pages and, for concepts no topic lists, from the catalogue Level 1, 2 and 3 fields through a published mapping table. Each heo:classifiedUnder link is also stated as skos:broader, so SKOS tools that do not reason still show one tree. To read only is-type-of links, take the skos:broader links that are not also heo:classifiedUnder links.
