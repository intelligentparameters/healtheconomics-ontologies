controlled vocabulary

https://healtheconomics.wiki/ontologies/helo/HELO_8000002

The learning topic of controlled vocabularies, lists of explicitly enumerated terms kept by an authority that registers them, in which each term has one unambiguous and non-redundant definition, a design goal that may not be met in practice. At least a term used for different concepts is qualified, and one of several terms for the same thing is chosen as the preferred term with the others as synonyms, as the concepts of the Health Economics Ontology (HEO) do with their preferred and alternative labels.

Type learning topic
definition source NISO 2005, section 4
isDefinedBy heloexternal
source Baines D. Scope of the learning pathways ontology, 18 October 2026, passed on by Michelle Ashley in the HELO 0.2.0 brief, building block 1 of 6 of the semantic layer

The learning topic of taxonomies, collections of controlled vocabulary terms arranged in a hierarchy in which each term has broader or narrower terms, such as the Knowledge Tree of health economics with its subdomains and topics.

thesaurus

https://healtheconomics.wiki/ontologies/helo/HELO_8000005

The learning topic of thesauri, controlled vocabularies arranged in a known order whose equivalence, hierarchical and associative relationships between terms (use and used for, broader and narrower, related) are shown by standard relationship indicators used in both directions, such as a vocabulary recording QALY as an equivalent of quality-adjusted life year.
