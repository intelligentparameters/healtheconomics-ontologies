Health Economics Learning Ontology (HELO)

The Health Economics Learning Ontology (HELO) states the learning pathways of HealthEconomics.wiki as an ontology, ordered learning steps that build understanding of HEO concepts from foundational to advanced, with their pathway frameworks, intended learner profiles, learning goals and the prerequisite relationships proposed from HEO, and gives courses such as the semantic layer course a shared structure of curricula and course modules. Learning pathways build understanding, not hands-on skills. They prepare a learner for the tasks of the Health Economics Task Ontology (HETO) and do not train them. Classes are information entities of IAO under BFO 2020, and the Curriculum KG Ontology and schema.org are mapped in SSSOM, not imported. HELO here is the Health Economics Learning Ontology of HealthEconomics.wiki. It is not the HypothEsis and Law Ontology, also called HELO (Soldatova et al. 2013), and is unrelated to it.

concept understanding goal

https://healtheconomics.wiki/ontologies/helo/HELO_0000007
Curation status pending final vettingexternal

A learning goal that states which concepts, health economics concepts or learning topics, a learner is intended to understand, and that is met by remembering and understanding them, including how methods work (interpreting, exemplifying, classifying, summarising, inferring, comparing and explaining), not by carrying out a procedure. It is the only kind of learning goal a learning pathway has.

Subclass of learning goal
Disjoint with task capability goal
Records of this type 51
In domain of concerns concept
contributor Michelle Ashleyexternal
definition source Baines D. Scope of the learning pathways ontology, 18 October 2026, passed on by Michelle Ashley in the HELO 0.2.0 brief; Krathwohl 2002
editor note Remember and Understand in the revised Bloom taxonomy (Krathwohl 2002). Explaining can be harder than executing, so understanding goals are a different kind of goal, not a lower tier. A goal at the Analyse or Evaluate level is typed by how it is met, by understanding concepts or by carrying out a procedure on a case.

learning pathway

https://healtheconomics.wiki/ontologies/helo/HELO_0000001
Curation status pending final vettingexternal

A directive information entity that sets out an ordered series of learning steps through which a learner builds understanding of related health economics concepts, from foundational concepts to more advanced ones. A learning pathway prepares a learner for health economics tasks but does not train the learner to carry them out.

Subclass of directive information entityexternal
Restrictions has learning step some learning step
has learning goal only concept understanding goal
Records of this type 8
In domain of builds on concept, covers subdomain, has display order, has learning step, has onward concept, leads into step, prepares for task
In range of agrees with order of, conflicts with order of, is framework of, is learning step of, is within one step of
alternative label pathway
contributor Michelle Ashleyexternal
definition source Baines D. Scope of the learning pathways ontology, 18 October 2026, passed on by Michelle Ashley in the HELO 0.2.0 brief; Ashley M. How the knowledge map works, notes for the site developer, 12 October 2026
editor note Darrin Baines's scope of 18 October 2026. Pathways help people understand concepts in a logical order from core to advanced, and they do not teach how to carry out tasks. The line follows the revised Bloom taxonomy (Krathwohl 2002). A pathway may aim at remembering and understanding, including explaining how a method works, which is understanding of procedural knowledge, and it may not train the carrying out of a procedure. A pathway is a plan of study, not a HETO task. HELO keeps the two apart in three ways. A learning pathway has only concept understanding goals (an OWL restriction and a SHACL rule), its relationship to a HETO task is prepares for task and never one of training, and a learning pathway may not also be typed as a HETO task specification (a SHACL rule). The Curriculum KG learning path is close in meaning and is mapped in SSSOM, not used as a parent, because its axioms require every learning step to have exactly one next step and one previous step, which a last step does not have.
has learner disclaimer Learning pathways build understanding, not hands-on skills.

term editor Michelle Ashley (drafted with Claude)

prerequisite relationship

https://healtheconomics.wiki/ontologies/helo/HELO_0000009
Curation status pending final vettingexternal

A data entity recording that understanding one HEO concept is needed, or proposed to be needed, before another HEO concept can be understood, with the HEO relationships it was derived from, its status and whether the learning pathways order the two concepts the same way.

Subclass of data entityexternal
Records of this type 391
In domain of agrees with order of, conflicts with order of, follows derivation rule, has dependent concept, has HEO link identifier, has prerequisite concept, has prerequisite status, is within one step of
alternative label prerequisite record
contributor Michelle Ashleyexternal
definition source HELO 0.2.0 design (best practice), Michelle Ashley with Claude, 19 October 2026; HEO 2.0.0
editor note A record, so a data entity, as the family puts its records. In HELO 0.2.0 every prerequisite relationship is a proposal derived from HEO relationships and none is asserted as fact. Proposals are not transitive for learning beyond one step, so a long chain is not a required route. A learning pathway itself asserts only the order of its steps and the concepts it builds on. Each record names the pathways whose order agrees with it, the pathways whose order conflicts with it and the pathways that put both concepts in one step.
