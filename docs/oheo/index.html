Outcomes Health Economics Ontology (OHEO)

An ontology of health outcomes as they are specified, measured, valued and summarised for health economics and health technology assessment. OHEO keeps four kinds of thing apart: outcome domains (what is measured, aligned to the 38 domains of the COMET outcome taxonomy), outcome measurement instruments (how it is measured; each named instrument is a class whose instances are its versions), valuations (utility functions and value sets, each named value set a class whose instances are its releases), and summary measures (QALY, DALY and related estimates). Outcome specifications, records of individual measurements, valuation methods and the reference cases of decision-making bodies are described with them. Policy (agreed with Darrin Baines, 15 October 2026): HEO identifies concepts; RHEO and OHEO define types classified within BFO 2020 (ISO/IEC 21838-2). A concept and a type have different identifiers even when their names are alike, and no OHEO class uses a HEO concept URI. HEO is the thesaurus of concepts; RHEO and OHEO classify kinds of things and do not overlap where they are used operationally (OHEO declares its records disjoint from the RHEO records they sit beside). SKOS names broker between them: the mappings to HEO concepts and RHEO types are published in oheo-mappings.sssom.tsv with justified, non-exact predicates, and a mapping never states identity. Requirements that records and analyses must meet, including the reference cases of NICE and nine other bodies, are published as SHACL shapes (oheo-shapes.ttl and oheo-reference-case-shapes.ttl), because OWL's open-world semantics cannot treat missing information as an error.
