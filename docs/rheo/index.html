Health Economics Ontologies

Real Health Economics Ontology (RHEO)

An ontology of health economic reality and of the evidence built from it. It describes the persons, organizations, households, resources and care settings involved in health care; what happens to and between them (care processes, resource use, patient movements, payments, behaviours, clinical events and changes of state); the records that describe these things (state observations and transitions, classification revisions, resource use, costs under a perspective, tariffs, payments, stocks and flows); and the population specifications, estimands, estimates, uncertainty, assumptions, sources, model parameters, parameter assignments, model versions and results built from them. It allows a model result to be traced to the estimates and assumptions it used and to the documents those estimates came from. RHEO is aligned to BFO 2020 (ISO/IEC 21838-2) and reuses IAO, OBI, COB, STATO, OGMS, OMRSE, RO and PROV-O terms. Its links to the Health Economics Ontology (HEO) are published as an SSSOM mapping file. Policy: HEO identifies concepts and organises their meanings with SKOS; RHEO defines types, each classified within BFO, with its own IRI, name, definition and relationships. A HEO concept and a RHEO type have different identities even when their names are alike, and a RHEO type never uses a HEO concept URI as its identifier. The RNI typology of changes of state is kept in a separate module (the reading adopted for 0.3.0) (rheo-rni.ttl), and the dependence between uncertain quantities in an extension module (rheo-uncertainty.ttl); the core does not depend on either.
