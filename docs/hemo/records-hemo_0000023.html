Flagship three-state Markov cohort model structure

https://healtheconomics.wiki/ontologies/hemo/HEMO_5000894
Curation status pending final vettingexternal

The equations of the three-state Stable, Progressed and Dead cohort model of the Build demonstrator, version 1.1, for one arm. Transition probabilities come from a progression probability, a relative risk of progression and death probabilities. The cohort trace is updated once a cycle, the trapezoidal half-cycle correction gives the occupancy of each cycle, and costs and QALYs are weighted by the cycle length and discounted at the start of each cycle.

Type model structure
definition source Sonnenberg and Beck 1993; Siebert et al. 2012; Naimark et al. 2008; Elbasha and Chhatwal 2016; Drummond et al. 2015
editor note A HEMO-native record, for a formula the Functions and Formulae module does not yet hold. It is listed in module-backlog.md for the module curator.
The structure corrects the occupancy of each cycle by the trapezoid rule and discounts each cycle at its start, the Build demonstrator's choices. Mid-cycle discounting is the usual pairing with the trapezoid. A uniform shift of the discount timing scales costs and QALYs alike and leaves the ICER at GBP 29,359.56, while dropping the correction moves it to GBP 31,832.90, as reviewer B recomputed on 19 October 2026. The QALY sum applies the trapezoid to occupancy at constant utility in each state, the form of HE-FM-QALY-001 discounted, not the trapezoid in utility of HE-FM-QALY-002.
