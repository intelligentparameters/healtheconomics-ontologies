Health Economics Ontologies

Health Economics Mathematics Ontology (HEMO)

First release (status alpha) for review by its creators and three independent reviewers. HEMO imports OHEO 0.3.0, RHEO 0.4.0 with its uncertainty module, which imports BFO 2020 (ISO/IEC 21838-2), and the five linking kinds of HEAO 0.2.0 as a small extract, so that HEMO does not import all of HEAO for them. The BFO, IAO, COB, OBI and STATO terms RHEO and OHEO declare are reused. IAO measurement unit label and datum label are declared here from IAO. Term IRIs follow the family pattern https://healtheconomics.wiki/ontologies/hemo/HEMO_ and seven digits. Load hemo-formulae.owl for the formula catalogue.
