Health Economics Ontologies

HealthEconomics.wiki publishes eight linked ontologies for health economics. HEO holds the concepts of the field, and each of the other seven covers one part of it. Each ontology has a page that lists every term in the current release with its definition and links.

Health Economics Ontology (HEO)

Release 2.0.0

The concepts of health economics, with definitions and typed links between them, taken from the wiki.

Real Health Economics Ontology (RHEO)

Release 0.4.0

The real things health economics works with, such as patients, resources, care processes and payments, and the records made about them.

Outcomes Health Economics Ontology (OHEO)

Release 0.3.0

Health outcomes as they are specified, measured, valued and summarised.

Health Economics Task Ontology (HETO)

Release 0.3.0

Units of health economics work, such as adding discounting to a model, and the inputs and outputs of each.

Health Economics Axiology Ontology (HEAO)

Release 0.2.0

How quantities are turned into values, such as health into QALYs or resources into costs.

Health Economics Mathematics Ontology (HEMO)

Release 0.2.0

The formulas, variables, assumptions and tests the other ontologies rely on.

Health Economics Learning Ontology (HELO)

Release 0.2.0

Learning pathways through the HEO concepts, ordered by level.

Health Economics Institutions and Rules Ontology (HEIRO)

Release 0.2.0

The institutions and rules of health economics and HTA, the bodies that issue them and when they apply.

Files, licence and citation details are in the GitHub repository. The ontology pages on the wiki are at healtheconomics.wiki/ontologies.
